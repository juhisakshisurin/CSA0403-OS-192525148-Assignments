VIDEO STREAMING START-UP: TWO-DESIGN SIMULATION
Model inputs:
- 10,000 playback-start/segment requests.
- 1,000 requests/second average arrival rate (0.5..1.5 ms intervals).
- 24 HDD service lanes, each random read 8..10 ms.
- Hybrid design: 4 SSD lanes, each read 0.25..0.40 ms.
- Hot-cache hit probability: 85%; misses are served by HDD.
- One request represents the first segment read for playback.
- These are simulated parameters, not physical benchmark values.

Note: queueing is represented by earliest-available device lanes.
