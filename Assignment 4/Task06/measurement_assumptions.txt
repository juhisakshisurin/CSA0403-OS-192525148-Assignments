ASSIGNMENT 4 - TASK 6: DESIGN AND SIMULATION ASSUMPTIONS

SOURCE-GIVEN
- 200,000 students.
- Each video size is 300 MB to 2 GB.
- Videos are uploaded once.
- Videos are read many thousands of times, mostly during the first week.
- Students may seek to any minute.
- Budget supports a few servers with HDDs and SSDs; cloud storage may be used.

DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS (NOT PROVIDED BY QUESTION)
- 1,000 videos in the catalogue.
- 1 GiB average video size.
- 2% simultaneous viewers = 4,000.
- 8 Mbit/s average stream bitrate.
- 20% throughput headroom.
- 4-second video segments.
- 4 KiB filesystem blocks.
- 1 MiB initial read-ahead.
- 3 origin nodes, each 8 x 8 TB HDD RAID10 and 2 x 1.92 TB NVMe RAID1.
- Replication factor 2 across nodes.
- 85% hot-cache hit ratio for simulator.
- 24 HDD service lanes, 8-10 ms random-read service time.
- 4 SSD service lanes, 0.25-0.40 ms service time.
- 10,000 playback request simulation at about 1,000 requests/second.

PERFORMANCE TARGETS (DESIGN TARGETS, NOT SOURCE FACTS)
- p95 playback start <= 2 seconds for campus clients.
- p95 remote playback start <= 4 seconds.
- p95 seek-to-play <= 2 seconds.
- Rebuffering <= 1% of playback time.
- 99.9% service availability target.

SIMULATION LIMITATIONS
- Single-server lane pools model storage queues only.
- No TCP/IP, HTTP, CDN, codec, CPU, encryption or real device firmware is modelled.
- Service times are assumed, not measured from physical devices.
- Cache hit rate is fixed at 85%, not measured from popularity logs.
- Results compare relative behaviour under this model only.
